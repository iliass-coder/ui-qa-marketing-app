Support, when you need it.

Questions about your account, campaign checks, or subscription? Contact us with the details and we will help you find the next step.

Contact customer support

Email iliass.elamrani@outlook.com. Include your workspace email, the affected campaign or page, and approximately when the issue occurred. Please keep passwords, access tokens, and payment card details out of your message.

Account access

Use password recovery if you cannot sign in with a password. If you use Google, choose Google sign-in with the same account you used when creating your workspace.

You can change your password and manage your Meta connection in Settings. If Meta access expires, reconnect and approve the permissions needed by the features you use.

Billing and cancellations

Open Plan & usage to review your allowances, invoices, and subscription. The Manage subscription & invoices button opens Stripe's customer portal when a billing customer is available.

Subscription cancellation through the portal takes effect at the end of the paid billing period. For a billing question or refund request, contact support with the invoice reference and workspace email.

Campaign checks and monitoring

Checks identify supported differences between Meta ads and their landing pages. A finding can be uncertain or incomplete and may require your review. Scheduled inspections depend on your plan, active Meta access, available capacity, and service availability.

Automatic campaign pausing is optional. It requires explicit opt-in, campaign management permission, and confirmed page failures. Simulation mode records proposed pauses without changing campaigns.

Privacy and data deletion requests

For access to your data, correction, deletion, or a privacy question, email support from the account address or include enough information for us to identify the workspace. We may need to verify your authority before acting on a request.

Account deletion is available in Settings and requires email confirmation. Deleting the final workspace member also removes the workspace's application data and cancels an associated subscription before deletion. Historical data belonging to a shared workspace can remain when other members still use it.

Disconnecting Meta removes the stored connection credentials. It does not delete imported reports, and it does not change your campaigns in Meta.
