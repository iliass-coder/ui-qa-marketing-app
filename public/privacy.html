Privacy notice

Effective and last updated October 25, 2026.

Who is responsible

Elamrani Technologies Inc., Canada ("we", "us", or "our"), operates Click Cohere at www.clickcohere.com. Our privacy contact can be reached at iliass.elamrani@outlook.com for questions, requests, or complaints about information under our control.

Information we process

  • Account information: your name, email address, workspace details, verification status, and authentication records. Passwords are stored as hashes. Optional Google sign-in supplies profile and verified email information; we do not receive your Google password.
  • Connected Meta information: authorized connection credentials, selected ad accounts, campaign and ad data, destination URLs, permissions, and access expiry information. We access information made available through the permissions you authorize.
  • Campaign and page evidence: ad copy, landing-page content, inspection results, screenshots where captured, monitoring baselines, reports, and incidents produced by the checks you request or enable.
  • Billing information: Stripe customer and subscription references, plan and payment status, billing periods, payment or invoice references, and usage allowances. Payment card details are collected by Stripe on its hosted payment surfaces.
  • Technical and support information: session information, request and error logs, security events, job status, audit entries, and information you include in support messages. Hosting and integration providers may also process network information such as IP addresses to operate and protect their services.

How and why we use information

We use this information to provide account access, connect services you authorize, inspect campaigns and pages, deliver reports and notifications, manage subscriptions and usage, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, understand onboarding and service use, and meet applicable legal obligations.

For service measurement, we record pseudonymous workspace identifiers and limited outcomes such as workspace creation, viewing a completed QA report, successful monitoring inspections, and confirmed subscription status. These measurements exclude names, email addresses, passwords, connection credentials, ad copy, and inspected page contents.

Where consent is required, we seek it for the relevant collection, use, or disclosure. Connecting an integration authorizes the requested connection; you can disconnect it in Settings. You can contact us to withdraw consent, subject to applicable legal or contractual restrictions. We will explain the consequences, including when a feature or the service can no longer be provided.

Where other privacy laws apply and require a specific legal basis, processing may be necessary to perform our agreement with you, meet a legal obligation, protect legitimate interests in operating and securing the service where permitted, or act on your consent.

Service providers and integrations

We share the information needed for the following functions:

  • Render: application hosting and PostgreSQL database storage.
  • Resend: delivery of account, security, and monitoring emails, including their recipients and message contents.
  • Stripe: payment processing, invoices, subscriptions, and customer billing management.
  • PostHog, US region: limited website and product analytics, including pseudonymous identifiers, conversion milestones, monitoring continuity, and recurring subscription amounts. Our server forwards these events without visitors' IP addresses. Session recording and automatic click or form capture are not enabled.
  • Google: optional account sign-in.
  • Meta: authorized advertising data access and optional campaign management actions.
  • OpenAI's API: selected ad copy and landing-page evidence for configured semantic comparisons.

Only connect accounts and submit campaign or page information that you are authorized to use for these purposes. Integrations may also process information independently under their own terms and privacy policies. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when lawfully necessary to address fraud, security threats, or legal claims.

Processing outside Canada

The application and its database are currently hosted in Virginia, United States. Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Information processed outside Canada may be subject to those countries' laws and accessible to their courts, law enforcement, or other public authorities. We remain responsible for personal information under our control and use applicable contractual and other safeguards for processing on our behalf. Contact our privacy contact for information about these arrangements.

Cookies and browser storage

The application uses cookies for sign-in sessions and the OAuth connection process. Browser session storage remembers a selected pricing plan during registration. Blocking essential cookies can prevent sign-in and connected features from working.

Optional browser analytics run after you choose Allow analytics. We store your preference and a random visitor identifier for up to 180 days to measure visits and signup conversion. Only the page category, campaign labels, and referring domain are recorded; full URLs and recovery tokens are not sent. Choose Privacy choices on the application to allow or decline future browser analytics. This preference does not disable the limited server-side workspace and billing measurements described above.

Retention and deletion

We retain personal information for as long as needed for the purposes described in this notice, including providing your workspace, handling requests or disputes, and satisfying applicable legal obligations. The application schedules deletion of unreferenced raw monitoring snapshots older than 30 days; approved baselines and evidence referenced by reports or incidents are preserved while needed for the workspace.

Account deletion is available in Settings and requires email confirmation. Deleting the final workspace member also removes the workspace's application data after any associated subscription is canceled. Shared workspace history can remain for other members. Disconnecting Meta removes stored connection credentials but does not delete imported reports or change campaigns in Meta.

Stripe and other providers may retain their own records, including payment and accounting records, under their policies and applicable legal obligations. A deletion request does not require information to be removed where retention is required by law.

Your requests and concerns

You can ask about the personal information we hold, request access or correction, request deletion where available, withdraw consent as described above, or raise a concern by emailing our privacy contact. We may verify your identity and authority to protect the workspace before acting. We respond within applicable legal time limits and explain any lawful restriction on a request.

If a concern is not resolved, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the provincial or other privacy authority that has jurisdiction. Additional rights, such as objection, restriction, or portability, may apply under the law applicable to you.

Security and updates

The application uses HTTPS, hashed passwords and session tokens, encrypted stored Meta credentials, and workspace access controls. No security measure eliminates every risk. Contact support promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

We update this notice when our practices change and show the effective date above. When required, we provide additional notice or obtain consent before making a material change to how personal information is used.
