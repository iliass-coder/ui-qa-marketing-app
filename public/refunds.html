Refunds & cancellation

Effective and last updated October 25, 2026.

This policy applies to Click Cohere purchases from Elamrani Technologies Inc., Canada. Contact iliass.elamrani@outlook.com about cancellation or a refund.

Stop the next renewal

Sign in, open Plan & usage, and choose Manage subscription & invoices. Cancel the subscription in Stripe's customer portal. Portal cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid billing period; paid coverage continues through that date, subject to plan and account restrictions.

If you cannot access the portal, email support from your account email and ask us to cancel. A portal cancellation does not automatically issue a refund.

End service early with a prorated refund

You may request cancellation before the current paid billing period ends and receive a refund for the unused portion. This applies to the current paid period of an initial subscription or a renewal. Email support and state that you want an earlier cancellation and a prorated refund.

The effective cancellation date is the date we receive your request, or a later date you specify within the current paid billing period. The unused portion is measured from that effective date to the end of the paid period. Paid access ends on the effective cancellation date; support processing time does not move the start of the refund calculation.

Calculation: subscription amount actually paid for the period × unused time in that period ÷ total time in that period. Discounts and any previous credits or refunds are taken into account; applicable taxes are adjusted as required.

Example: if you paid $39 before tax for a 30-day period and 15 days remain at the effective cancellation date, the prorated refund is $19.50 before any applicable tax adjustment.

Time that has already elapsed and completed billing periods are not refundable under this proportional policy. Canceling without requesting an earlier end date keeps access until period end and creates no unused subscription time to refund. Mandatory legal rights still apply.

Purchased analysis packs

You may request a proportional refund for the unused purchased analyses in an unexpired pack. The amount is the pack price actually paid × unused purchased analyses ÷ analyses originally credited. Analyses already used or reserved for running work are excluded from the calculation until that work is finalized. Unused included subscription analyses have no separate cash value.

For example, if a pack of 100 analyses cost $15 and 40 remain unused, the proportional refund is $6 before any applicable tax adjustment. Refunded credits are no longer available for use. Purchased packs expire 90 days after crediting; expired or already used credits are not refundable under this policy unless applicable law requires otherwise.

How to request and receive a refund

Email iliass.elamrani@outlook.com with your workspace email, invoice or payment reference, the purchase concerned, and the cancellation date you want. We may verify account ownership and review payment and usage records. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or payment card details.

We confirm the calculation and process the eligible refund through the original payment provider to the original payment method where supported. Bank and payment-provider processing times vary. A refund request is handled by support; it is not an automatic action in the application or Stripe portal. Refunds cannot exceed the amount paid for the purchase after previous refunds.

Free preview and account deletion

The free preview requires no payment card and does not automatically charge when it ends. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are different actions. Deleting the last workspace member cancels the associated subscription before workspace deletion, but you must contact support to request a proportional refund.

Applicable rights

This policy supplements the Terms of service. It does not limit any cancellation, refund, warranty, or consumer right that applicable law does not allow us to exclude. Where mandatory law provides a greater remedy, that law applies.
