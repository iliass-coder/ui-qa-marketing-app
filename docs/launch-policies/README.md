# Policy drafts for ClickCohere

These pages are review drafts, kept outside `public/` so they cannot be published by an ordinary frontend build. The live support page is `public/support.html`.

Before publication, supply:

- The actual legal operator/business name and relevant business contact details.
- The refund policy, including any eligibility period, exclusions, and treatment of analysis packs.
- Any jurisdiction-specific information that applies to the operator and customers.

Replace the draft placeholders, move the approved pages into `public/`, permit those exact GET paths in Spring Security, add links in the website footer, and deploy the reviewed files. The existing `public/policies.css` supplies their styling.

The drafts describe the implemented product. They do not certify compliance with every applicable law or create an unapproved refund commitment.
