Information processed

Account information includes your name, email address, workspace details, verification status, and authentication records. Passwords are stored as hashes. Google sign-in supplies profile and verified email information; the service does not receive your Google password.

When you connect Meta, the service processes authorized connection credentials, selected ad accounts, campaign and ad data, destination URLs, and permission and expiry information. Landing-page checks collect page content and evidence needed to produce reports, monitoring baselines, and incidents.

Billing records include Stripe customer and subscription references, plan and payment status, billing periods, invoices or payment references, and usage allowances. Card details are collected on Stripe-hosted payment surfaces.

Technical records can include session information, request and error logs, security events, job status, and audit entries. Messages you send to support are processed to respond to your request.

The information is used to provide account access, connect authorized services, inspect campaigns and pages, deliver reports and notifications, manage subscriptions and usage, prevent misuse, and answer support requests. {{CONFIRMED_APPLICABLE_LEGAL_BASES_AND_JURISDICTION_INFORMATION}}

Service providers and integrations

Render hosts the application and its PostgreSQL database. Resend delivers transactional email. Stripe processes payments and subscription management. Google provides optional sign-in. Meta supplies the advertising information you authorize.

Configured semantic checks send selected ad copy and landing-page evidence to OpenAI's API to produce comparisons. Avoid including information in campaign or page content that you are not authorized to submit for this processing. Provider processing and retention are also governed by the applicable provider agreements and policies.

Cookies and browser storage

The application uses cookies for sign-in sessions and the OAuth handshake. Browser session storage remembers a selected pricing plan during registration. The current application does not embed advertising analytics or tracking pixels.

Retention

The application prunes unreferenced raw monitoring snapshots older than 30 days through a scheduled maintenance task. Approved baselines and evidence referenced by reports or incidents are preserved. Account, workspace, reporting, and billing-reference data are retained while needed to provide the service, meet applicable obligations, or address a request or dispute.

Workspace deletion removes application data for the final workspace member. Stripe and other providers can retain their own records under their policies and applicable obligations. {{CONFIRMED_BACKUP_RETENTION_AND_INTERNATIONAL_TRANSFER_INFORMATION}}
